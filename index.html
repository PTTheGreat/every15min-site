every15min

terminal-style capture log · for iPhone

what just happened?

Every 15 minutes, write one line. No categories, no timers, no friction. The timestamp, the rhythm, and the day's picture take care of themselves.

09:02 deep work: widget timeline provider #code
09:44 quick break, stretched, refilled water
10:01 standup: demoed the sparkline #team

features

  • git-log-style timeline, color-coded by time of day
  • stats bar: next window, gap, today's count, streak
  • 24-hour activity sparkline
  • #hashtags and full-history search
  • command console (:help, :find, :theme, :export …)
  • six dark themes · home screen widget · photo attachments

private by design

Your log never leaves your device. No account, no server, no analytics, no tracking. Export everything as JSON any time — optionally encrypted with AES-256. See the privacy policy.
