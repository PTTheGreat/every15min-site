工作方式How it works

记录、回看、找回，三步看清自己的节奏Capture, review, retrieve: three steps to see your rhythm

这是一种被称为“间隔日志”（interstitial journaling）的方法：不追求精确计时，只在每个时间间隙留下一句话。坚持几天，一天的真实形状就会出现。

It is a form of interstitial journaling: no stopwatch, just one sentence in each gap of your day. After a few days, the real shape of your time becomes visible.

常见问题FAQ

关于 every15minAbout every15min

every15min 是什么？What is every15min?

every15min 是一款 iPhone 时间记录 App。你每隔大约 15 分钟写下一行刚刚做了什么，App 会自动按时间排成一天的时间线，方便回看、搜索和复盘。

every15min is a time log app for iPhone. Roughly every 15 minutes you write one line about what you just did, and the app arranges it into a timeline you can review, search and reflect on.

必须严格每 15 分钟记录一次吗？Do I have to log exactly every 15 minutes?

不必。15 分钟是一个建议节奏，App 会显示下一个时间窗和距离上一条的间隔。错过的时间段会如实留空，不会被自动补全。

No. Fifteen minutes is a suggested rhythm; the app shows the next window and the gap since your last entry. Missed periods simply stay empty and are never filled in for you.

我的数据存在哪里？需要注册账号吗？Where is my data stored? Do I need an account?

所有记录、照片和设置都只保存在你的 iPhone 上。every15min 没有账号、服务器、同步服务、广告或分析工具，App 本身不发起网络请求。

Entries, photos and settings live only on your iPhone. There is no account, server, sync service, advertising or analytics, and the app makes no network requests.

可以导出或备份数据吗？Can I export or back up my data?

可以。你可以随时把记录导出为 JSON 文件，并可选择用口令加密（PBKDF2 + AES-GCM 256）。导入时在本机合并。删除 App 会同时删除本地数据，请保留导出文件作为备份。

Yes. Export to JSON at any time, optionally encrypted with a passphrase (PBKDF2 + AES-GCM 256). Imports merge locally. Deleting the app deletes its data, so keep an export as your backup.

every15min is a one-time purchase of US$1 on the App Store (local pricing may vary by region). No subscription, no ads, no tracking.
