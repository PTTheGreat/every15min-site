隐私政策Privacy policy

适用于 every15min iOS 应用 · 生效日期：2026 年 8 月 16 日Applies to every15min for iOS · Effective 2026-08-16

简要说明

every15min 不收集任何数据。你写下的一切都保存在你的设备上。

数据存储

你的记录、照片和设置均存储在你的 iPhone 本地。应用不发起网络请求：我们没有服务器、账号或同步服务，也没有分析工具、广告和第三方 SDK。

导出和导入

你可以随时将记录导出为 JSON 文件，并可选择使用口令加密（PBKDF2 + AES-GCM 256）。导出文件在你的设备上创建，仅分享到你自行选择的位置。导入功能只读取你选中的文件，并在本地合并。

照片

当你为记录添加照片时，应用仅访问你明确选择的照片，并将其存储在应用的本地数据库中。照片不会上传到任何地方。

我们能看到什么

什么也看不到。我们无法读取、接收或恢复你的数据。删除应用会同时删除其本地数据；如需备份，请保留一份导出文件。

本网站

以上内容只针对 App。本网站（every15min.tokendance.life）使用 Google Analytics 统计访问量和访问来源（例如来自搜索引擎还是其他网站）。它已设置为不使用 Cookie，并关闭了 Google 信号和广告个性化。我们只看汇总数据，不会用来识别任何人。（2026 年 10 月 22 日更新）

政策变更

如本政策发生变更，新版本会发布在此页面，并更新生效日期。

The short version

every15min collects no data. Everything you write stays on your device.

Data storage

Your entries, photos, and settings are stored locally on your iPhone. The app makes no network requests: there is no server, no account, no sync service operated by us, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.

Export and import

You can export your log as a JSON file at any time, optionally encrypted with a passphrase (PBKDF2 + AES-GCM 256). Export files are created on your device and shared only where you choose to send them. Import reads files you select and merges them locally.

Photos

If you attach photos to entries, the app accesses only the photos you explicitly pick, and stores them inside the app's local database. They are never uploaded anywhere.

What we can see

Nothing. We have no way to read, receive, or recover your data. If you delete the app, its local data is deleted with it — keep an export if you want a backup.

This website

Everything above is about the app. This website (every15min.tokendance.life) uses Google Analytics to count visits and see where they come from (for example, a search engine or another site). It runs without cookies, with Google signals and ad personalization turned off. We only look at aggregate numbers and never use them to identify anyone. (Updated 2026-10-22)

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this page with an updated effective date.
